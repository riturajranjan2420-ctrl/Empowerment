FOR THE GIRL WHO KEEPS GOING

For You ✨

A little reminder for the road ahead.

A reminder

You are stronger
than you think.

Zindagi hamesha easy nahi hogi. Kuch din beautiful honge, kuch confusing, aur kuch din aise bhi jab sab kuch heavy lagega.

TAP ANYWHERE
About life

Tough times
keep changing.

Aaj jo problem impossible lag rahi hai, zaroori nahi kal bhi utni hi badi lage.

Situations badalti hain. Log badalte hain. Tum bhi grow karti ho.

Don't let one difficult chapter define your whole story.

Take care of yourself

Life is precious
because it isn't guaranteed.

Health ho ya life ka koi aur part, kisi cheez ka future completely predict nahi kiya ja sakta.

Isliye dar kar jeena nahi... apni zindagi ko value dena.

Remember your value

Don't measure your worth
by one bad day.

Tumhari value marks, achievements, kisi ke opinion, ya kisi difficult phase se decide nahi hoti.

Tumhari value usse kahin zyada hai.

You are a person first.
Everything else comes after.

Keep your heart

Be strong.
But stay kind.

Strong hone ka matlab yeh nahi ki tumhe kabhi hurt nahi hona chahiye.

Rona aaye toh ro lena. Rest chahiye toh rest lena. Help chahiye toh help maang lena.

Phir jab ready ho... ek step aur aage badh jaana.

And please remember

Your life
belongs to you.

Apne dreams ko seriously lena.
Apni peace ko protect karna.
Apni health ka khayal rakhna.

Aur apni khushi ko hamesha kisi aur ki permission ka wait mat karwana.

One last thing

Keep going,
even if slowly.

Har din extraordinary hona zaroori nahi.

Kabhi-kabhi bas uthna, khud ka khayal rakhna, aur next day tak pahunch jaana bhi enough hota hai.

Your story is still being written ✦
